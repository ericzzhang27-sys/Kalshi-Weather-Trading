# Feature Candidate Backlog

These are timestamp-safe candidates to test later. They are not automatically added to `outputs/final_feature_list.json`; each one needs the same chronological validation and leakage-review process before production use.

| feature name | formula or construction method | required raw columns | why it might help | available at t | leakage risk | priority | suggested feature group | implementation notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| remaining_warming_needed | forecast_high - max_temp_so_far | forecast_high, max_temp_so_far | Measures how much additional warming is needed for the forecast to verify. | yes if forecast_high and max_temp_so_far are known at t | low | high | forecast_relative | This is the sign-inverted form of an existing kept feature; test explicitly before replacing or adding. |
| forecast_progress_ratio | (current_temp - morning_temp) / max(1, forecast_high - morning_temp) | current_temp, morning_temp from observations at/before t, forecast_high | Normalizes daily warming progress by forecasted warming potential. | yes after morning_temp is observed | low-medium | high | observed_temperature_path | Define morning cutoff before modeling and handle early prediction times carefully. |
| hours_until_typical_high | typical_high_hour_by_season - current_hour | prediction_time, historical typical high hour table | Captures remaining daylight/warming window without full calendar overfit. | yes if typical table is trained on past dates only | low-medium | medium | time_season | Estimate typical high hour using only training or prior dates. |
| warming_rate_since_morning | (current_temp - morning_temp) / max(1, hours_since_morning) | current_temp, morning_temp, prediction_time | Direct measure of realized warming pace. | yes after morning observation exists | low | high | recent_temperature_changes | Use fixed morning timestamp and no interpolation from future observations. |
| max_temp_so_far_minus_current_temp | max_temp_so_far - current_temp | max_temp_so_far, current_temp | Shows whether the day has already peaked or is still warming. | yes | low | medium | observed_temperature_path | Equivalent sign to current_temp_minus_max_so_far; test which sign/shape is better. |
| forecast_high_percentile_for_dayofyear | forecast_high percentile versus historical forecast highs for nearby day-of-year using only prior dates | forecast_high, prediction date, historical forecast_high | Flags unusually aggressive or conservative forecasts for the season. | yes if history window excludes current/future dates | medium | medium | forecast_relative | Implement with expanding or walk-forward history; never fit percentiles on validation/test future rows. |
| current_temp_percentile_for_dayofyear_hour | current_temp percentile versus historical observed temps at same season/hour using only prior dates | current_temp, prediction_time, historical hourly observations | Contextualizes current temperature anomaly for the hour and season. | yes if historical reference excludes current/future dates | medium | medium | observed_temperature_path | Use train-only or expanding historical statistics. |
| dew_point_change_3h | dew_point_now - dew_point_3h_ago | dew_point current and 3-hour lag from hourly observations | Moisture trend may explain temperature forecast error and cloud persistence. | yes | low | medium | humidity_dew_point | Use same lag lookup rules as temperature changes. |
| empirical_baseline_mean_error | mean forecast error from eligible historical analogs using only previous dates | past forecast_error, safe analog features | Gives NGBoost a local bias prior. | yes only with strictly past-date analog store | medium-high | high | empirical_baseline | Compute walk-forward. Do not use same-year future validation/test errors. |
| empirical_baseline_error_sigma | forecast-error standard deviation from eligible historical analogs using only previous dates | past forecast_error, safe analog features | Gives NGBoost a local uncertainty prior. | yes only with strictly past-date analog store | medium-high | high | empirical_baseline | Compute walk-forward with minimum sample thresholds and no future dates. |

For historical percentile and empirical baseline features, compute the reference statistics with only dates before the prediction date. A global fit over all dates would leak validation/test information.
